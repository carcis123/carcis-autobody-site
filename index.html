Collision Repair, Paint, and Custom Auto Body Work in Vancouver, Washington

Carcis Autobody Repair is a certified auto body repair shop at 1520 Washington St in Vancouver, Washington, serving the Vancouver and Portland metro area. The shop handles collision repair, dent and panel repair, rust repair, and both standard and custom paint work, for customers going through an insurance claim as well as customers paying for repairs directly.

About This Shop's Services

Collision Repair

Carcis repairs vehicles after collisions, from minor fender-benders to major accident damage. The shop works directly with insurance companies on claims and also serves customers paying out of pocket. Read more on the services page.

Dent and Panel Repair

Dents and damaged panels are repaired to factory fit, or replaced when a panel cannot be brought back to spec. See the full breakdown on what Carcis fixes.

Rust Repair

Rust removal and panel restoration, including work on classic and older vehicles. This is one of owner Carlos Carrasco Cisneros's specialties; read about his background on the About page.

Paint and Custom Paint

Carcis offers full and partial paint jobs matched to factory color, plus fully custom paint designs and color work for owners who want something specific rather than a standard repaint.

Custom Body Work

Beyond standard repair, Carcis takes on custom body work for vehicle builds that need something specific done to them.

Insurance Claims or Personal Pay

Carcis serves both insurance-claim repairs and personal-pay repairs through the same estimate and repair process, with no difference in how a customer is treated either way.

Service Area

Based in Vancouver, WA, Carcis serves the surrounding Vancouver/Portland metro area, including Camas, Battle Ground, Hazel Dell, and Salmon Creek.

How to Book a Free Estimate

Estimates are free and can be booked directly on the Get an Estimate page, which opens a calendar for picking an available time. Carcis is open Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm.
