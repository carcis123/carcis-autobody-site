About Carcis

Built by someone who actually trained for this.

Carcis Autobody Repair is owned and run by Carlos Carrasco Cisneros, a certified auto body technician — not someone who picked up tools and called it a trade.

Since finishing school and earning his certification, Carlos has handled everything from routine dent and panel repair to full custom paint designs, total-loss vehicle rebuilds, and rust repair on classic cars. It's not a sideline skill — it's what he trained specifically to do, and it shows in the finish work.

Kayla Carrasco Cisneros co-owns and runs the business alongside him, keeping the shop running and the communication straight with every customer who walks in.

Carcis opened recently in Vancouver, WA, and that's on purpose. A smaller, newer shop that hasn't gotten backed up yet, run by people who answer the phone and actually explain what's going on with your car — not a call center, not a queue, just the two people who own the place.
