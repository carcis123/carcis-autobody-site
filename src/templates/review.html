About Carcis AI

It is a set of questions and a bit of sentence-joining, and we would rather describe it plainly than make it sound cleverer than it is. You tap the answers; it puts those answers into sentences. It cannot add a detail you did not give it, cannot talk us up, and cannot soften a complaint — if you tap “the finish was not right”, the draft says the finish was not right. Nothing you tap leaves your phone.

Then it is yours. The draft lands in a box you can rewrite completely, and nothing goes anywhere until you copy it over to Google yourself. If what comes out does not sound like you, change it or bin it — we would rather have five sentences in your own words than fifty in ours.
