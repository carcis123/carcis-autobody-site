About Carcis AI

Two separate things, and it's worth knowing which is which. The talking part is your own phone or browser turning speech into text — we don't record audio, we don't store it, and no sound reaches us. The tidy-up button is the only part that sends anything anywhere, and only when you press it.

What it will do is fix spelling, punctuation and the words your phone misheard. What it won't do is write your review. It can't add a detail you didn't mention, can't talk us up, and can't soften a complaint — if you say the paint didn't match, it still says the paint didn't match. You see both versions and yours is the one that stays unless you pick the other. We'd rather have five honest sentences than fifty polished ones.
